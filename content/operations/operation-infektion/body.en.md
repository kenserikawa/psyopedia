## Overview

Operation INFEKTION was a KGB disinformation campaign that spread the false claim that HIV/AIDS was engineered by the U.S. military at Fort Detrick as a biological weapon.

## Methods

- Planted the story in an Indian newspaper (Patriot) in 1983 as a "letter from an American scientist"
- Amplified through Soviet media and sympathetic outlets worldwide
- Recruited East German biophysicist Jakob Segal to give the claim scientific credibility
- The story spread to 80 countries in 30 languages by 1987

## Exposure

Soviet defectors confirmed the operation. Gorbachev reportedly ordered it stopped as part of glasnost.

## Legacy

Surveys show the myth persists: as late as 2005, 50% of African Americans believed AIDS was man-made. The operation is considered one of the most successful disinformation campaigns in history and a template for modern information warfare.
