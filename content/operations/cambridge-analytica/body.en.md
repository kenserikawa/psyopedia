## Overview

Cambridge Analytica harvested personal data from 87 million Facebook users without consent to build psychological profiles and target them with political ads.

## Methods

- Harvested data via a personality quiz app
- Built psychographic profiles based on OCEAN model
- Used techniques derived from military psychological operations

## Exposure

Whistleblower Christopher Wylie revealed the operation in March 2018.

## Consequences

Cambridge Analytica shut down in May 2018. Facebook was fined $5 billion by the FTC.
