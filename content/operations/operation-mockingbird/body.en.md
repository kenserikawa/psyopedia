## Overview

Operation Mockingbird was an alleged large-scale CIA campaign to infiltrate and influence American media organizations during the Cold War.

## Methods

- Direct recruitment of journalists as CIA assets
- Funding of student and cultural organizations as front groups
- Placement of stories in major publications

## Key Details

At its peak, the operation allegedly included over 400 journalists and 25 major organizations.

## Exposure

Details emerged through the Church Committee investigations in 1975.
