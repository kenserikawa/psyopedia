## Overview

The chemtrail theory alleges aircraft contrails are chemical agents deliberately sprayed.

## Evidence Status

**Thoroughly debunked.** A 2016 study of 77 atmospheric scientists found zero support.

## The Interesting Kernel

While chemtrails as described are fiction, real programs DO exist: cloud seeding, Operation LAC (1957-58), and geoengineering research.

## Why It Matters

Demonstrates how real but limited programs can be extrapolated into global conspiracy narratives.
