## Overview

Operation CHAOS (1967-1974) was a CIA domestic espionage program that spied on American anti-war activists and dissidents, violating the CIA's charter which prohibits domestic operations.

## Methods

- Compiled files on over 300,000 Americans
- Infiltrated anti-war organizations with undercover agents
- Indexed 7,200 individuals in a computerized database
- Coordinated with FBI's COINTELPRO for domestic targeting
- Agents provocateurs attended rallies and protests

## Exposure

Revealed by investigative journalist Seymour Hersh in 1974 and subsequently investigated by the Rockefeller Commission and Church Committee.

## Legacy

Led to Executive Order 12333 restricting CIA domestic activities. Less well-known than COINTELPRO despite similar scope and illegality.
