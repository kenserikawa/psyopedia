## Overview

The Panama Papers were 11.5 million leaked documents from Panamanian law firm Mossack Fonseca, revealing how world leaders, politicians, billionaires, and celebrities used offshore shell companies to hide wealth and evade taxes.

## The Leak

In 2015, an anonymous source provided the documents to German newspaper Süddeutsche Zeitung, which shared them with the International Consortium of Investigative Journalists (ICIJ). Over 400 journalists from 80 countries analyzed the data.

## Key Revelations

- 12 current or former world leaders implicated
- 128 politicians and public officials identified
- Connections to criminal networks, sanctions evasion, and fraud
- Iceland's Prime Minister resigned within days of publication
- Links to associates of Putin, the King of Saudi Arabia, and the president of Ukraine

## The Murder of Daphne Caruana Galizia

Maltese journalist Daphne Caruana Galizia, who investigated Panama Papers connections in Malta, was killed by a car bomb in October 2017. Three men were convicted; a Maltese businessman was charged as mastermind.

## Legacy

The leak led to over $1.36 billion in recovered taxes globally and prompted reforms in dozens of countries.
