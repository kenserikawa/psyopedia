## Overview

A cluster of conspiracy theories alleging that filmmaker Stanley Kubrick was involved in fabricating the Apollo moon landing footage and that his later films contain encoded confessions of this and other elite secrets.

## The Moon Landing Theory

The central claim alleges NASA hired Kubrick to film fake moon landing footage after his work on "2001: A Space Odyssey" (1968) demonstrated realistic space visuals. Proponents cite alleged visual anomalies in Apollo footage and Kubrick's known perfectionism.

## The Shining as Confession

The documentary "Room 237" (2012) popularized interpretations of "The Shining" as Kubrick's encoded confession:
- Danny's Apollo 11 sweater as a direct reference
- Room 237 representing the mean distance to the Moon (237,000 miles)
- Patterns in the carpet resembling a launch pad

## Eyes Wide Shut

Kubrick's final film, depicting elite secret rituals, fueled theories that he was exposing real practices of powerful secret societies. Kubrick died days after showing his final cut to Warner Bros., leading to claims he was silenced.

## Evidence Status

**No credible evidence.** The moon landing theory is thoroughly debunked by independent verification. Film interpretations rely on subjective pattern recognition. The "confession" video circulating online is a proven fabrication.

## Why It Matters

Illustrates how artistic ambiguity in films can be reinterpreted as hidden messages, and how the death of a public figure near a controversial work fuels conspiracy thinking.
