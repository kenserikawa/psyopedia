## Overview

Operation Paperclip was a secret United States intelligence program in which more than 1,600 German scientists, engineers, and technicians were recruited from post-Nazi Germany.

## Methods

- Whitewashing Nazi records to bypass President Truman's anti-Nazi directive
- Providing new identities and immigration paperwork
- Placing scientists in key positions at NASA, Fort Bliss, and other installations

## Key Figures

- Wernher von Braun — former SS officer, became director of NASA's Marshall Space Flight Center
- Kurt Blome — Nazi biological weapons scientist

## Legacy

Operation Paperclip fundamentally shaped U.S. space and military technology programs.
