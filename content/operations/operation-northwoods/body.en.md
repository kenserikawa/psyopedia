## Overview

Operation Northwoods was a proposed false flag operation against American citizens that originated within the U.S. Department of Defense in 1962.

## Proposals

- Staging mock attacks on U.S. military bases
- Sinking a U.S. Navy ship in Guantanamo Bay
- Hijacking planes and blaming Cuba
- Bombing Miami and Washington, D.C.

## Rejection

President John F. Kennedy rejected the proposal and removed Chairman Lyman Lemnitzer.

## Declassification

The documents were declassified in 1997 as part of the JFK Assassination Records Review Board process.
