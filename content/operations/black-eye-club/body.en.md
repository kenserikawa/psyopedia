## Overview

The Black Eye Club is a conspiracy theory noting that numerous world leaders, celebrities, and public figures have appeared in public with unexplained black left eyes, suggesting a secret initiation ritual or control mechanism.

## Notable Figures

Public figures photographed with black eyes include Pope Francis, George W. Bush, John McCain, Prince Philip, John Kerry, and numerous entertainment figures — all on the left eye.

## Theories

- **Ritual initiation** — An alleged secret society hazing involving the "soul scalping" or "Vril" injection through the left eye
- **Coincidence** — Skeptics note that black eyes are common injuries and confirmation bias selects for matching cases
- **Adrenochrome extraction** — Fringe claims connect it to alleged substance harvesting

## Evidence Status

**No credible evidence.** The theory relies entirely on photographic coincidence and pattern-seeking behavior. No whistleblower, document, or verifiable source supports the claims.

## Why It Matters

Demonstrates how confirmation bias and pattern recognition can construct elaborate narratives from unrelated events.
