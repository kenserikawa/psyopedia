## Overview

Operation Highjump (1946-47) was a U.S. Navy Antarctic expedition led by Admiral Richard Byrd involving 4,700 personnel, 13 ships, and 33 aircraft. Officially a training and research mission, conspiracy theories allege hidden objectives.

## Official Purpose

- Establish Antarctic research base Little America IV
- Train personnel for polar operations
- Map and photograph the continent
- Test equipment in extreme conditions

## Conspiracy Theories

- **Nazi base theory** — Alleges the mission targeted a secret Nazi base ("Base 211") in Neuschwabenland, allegedly established by the 1938-39 German expedition
- **Admiral Byrd's interview** — A 1947 Chilean newspaper interview quotes Byrd warning of "flying objects that travel at incredible speeds" from pole to pole, though the translation and context are disputed
- **Early termination** — The expedition ended months ahead of schedule; the official reason was Antarctic winter approaching, while theorists claim military casualties
- **Hollow Earth** — Fringe theories claim Byrd discovered an entrance to a hollow Earth

## Evidence Status

**The expedition is real and well-documented. The conspiracy theories have no credible evidence.** Naval records show a standard large-scale expedition. The early departure is consistent with weather and logistical challenges.
