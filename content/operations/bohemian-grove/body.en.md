## Overview

Bohemian Grove is a 2,700-acre campground in Monte Rio, California where the all-male Bohemian Club has held a two-week retreat every July since 1878. Attendees include U.S. presidents, military leaders, CEOs, and media moguls.

## Confirmed Facts

- The Manhattan Project was reportedly conceived at the Grove in 1942
- Richard Nixon and Ronald Reagan negotiated their presidential ticket there in 1967
- Past attendees include every Republican president since Coolidge, plus many Democrats
- The "Cremation of Care" ceremony involves burning an effigy before a 40-foot owl statue

## The Conspiracy

- Alex Jones infiltrated and filmed the Cremation of Care in 2000
- Theories range from elite networking (mundane) to occult rituals (extreme)
- Claims of policy decisions made outside democratic processes
- Allegations of Moloch worship based on the owl iconography

## Evidence Status

**Mixed.** The club, its membership, and the ceremony are real and documented. The occult interpretations are speculative. The influence-peddling concern — that world-shaping decisions are made at private retreats — is legitimate but not unique to the Grove.
