## Overview

Plan Andina refers to a series of U.S.-backed covert and overt operations across the Andean region, ostensibly aimed at counter-narcotics but allegedly used to influence domestic politics, suppress leftist movements, and secure strategic resources.

## Background

Beginning in the late 1980s, the U.S. expanded military aid and intelligence operations throughout the Andes under the banner of the War on Drugs. Leaked documents suggest the programs extended far beyond drug interdiction.

## Methods

- Funding paramilitary groups as proxies against guerrilla organizations
- Intelligence sharing with local military units linked to human rights abuses
- Economic pressure through conditional aid packages
- DEA and CIA coordination with local security forces

## Controversy

Critics argue Plan Andina was less about drugs and more about maintaining U.S. geopolitical influence in South America, particularly against left-leaning governments.
