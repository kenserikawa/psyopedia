## Overview

Operation PBSuccess was the 1954 CIA covert operation that overthrew Guatemala's democratically elected President Jacobo Árbenz, replacing him with military dictator Carlos Castillo Armas.

## Background

Árbenz's land reform program threatened United Fruit Company's vast landholdings. The CIA framed the operation as preventing Soviet influence in Central America.

## Methods

- CIA radio station "Voice of Liberation" broadcast fake reports of a large rebel army
- Psychological warfare leaflets dropped over Guatemala City
- Small paramilitary force trained in Honduras created illusion of overwhelming invasion
- Diplomatic pressure isolated Guatemala internationally
- Bombing runs by CIA pilots created panic

## Legacy

Triggered decades of civil war in Guatemala (1960-1996) resulting in 200,000 deaths. Became a template for future CIA operations in Latin America. The operation's "success" emboldened the CIA for the Bay of Pigs (which failed).
