## Overview

Operation Ajax was the CIA/MI6-orchestrated coup d'état that overthrew Iran's democratically elected Prime Minister Mohammad Mosaddegh in August 1953 and reinstated Shah Mohammad Reza Pahlavi.

## Background

Mosaddegh had nationalized Iran's oil industry, threatening British Petroleum's monopoly. The British enlisted the CIA after failing to mount the coup alone.

## Methods

- Bribed newspaper editors, mullahs, and politicians to turn public opinion against Mosaddegh
- Hired mobs to stage pro-Shah and anti-Mosaddegh street protests
- Planted false stories linking Mosaddegh to communism
- Coordinated with Iranian military officers for the final overthrow
- The initial coup attempt failed; a second attempt succeeded days later

## Exposure

The CIA publicly acknowledged its role in 2013 when declassified documents were released. Internal CIA histories had leaked earlier.

## Legacy

Widely considered a primary cause of the 1979 Iranian Revolution and anti-American sentiment in the Middle East. A foundational case study in CIA regime change operations.
