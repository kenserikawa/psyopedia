## Overview

Multiple governments have developed and deployed software that allows single operators to manage dozens of fake social media personas simultaneously, creating the illusion of grassroots support or opposition.

## Known Programs

- **U.S. CENTCOM (2011)** — HBGary Federal contract for "persona management software" leaked by Anonymous. Each operator could manage 10 fake identities with realistic backgrounds
- **GCHQ JTRIG (Snowden, 2014)** — British intelligence's Joint Threat Research Intelligence Group used "dirty tricks" including fake victim blog posts, fake posts on internet forums, and honey traps
- **China's 50 Cent Army** — Estimated 2 million people paid to post pro-government content. A 2017 Harvard study estimated 448 million fabricated social media posts per year
- **Israel's Act.IL (2017)** — App that crowdsources pro-Israel social media engagement through coordinated commenting campaigns

## Methods

- AI-assisted generation of personas with realistic histories
- VPN rotation to simulate geographic diversity
- Coordinated amplification of narratives across platforms
- Targeting of specific communities, journalists, and activists

## Evidence Status

**Well-documented through leaks.** Individual programs are confirmed through Snowden documents, FOIA requests, and leaked contracts. The full scale remains unknown.
