## Overview

Dead Internet Theory posits that the majority of internet traffic, content, and interactions are generated by bots and AI rather than real humans. The theory suggests this shift occurred around 2016-2017 and was either deliberately engineered or naturally emerged from algorithmic systems.

## Key Claims

- Most social media engagement comes from automated accounts
- AI-generated content has replaced organic human creation
- Search results are increasingly manipulated and artificial
- Online discourse is shaped by algorithmic amplification rather than genuine opinion

## Evidence For

- Studies estimate 40-60% of web traffic comes from bots
- Social media platforms regularly purge millions of fake accounts
- AI content generation has exploded since GPT-3 (2020)
- Engagement metrics are known to be heavily inflated by automation

## Evidence Against

- The theory overstates the proportion — humans still generate substantial content
- Bot traffic is mostly commercial (SEO, scraping) not ideological
- The conspiracy framing ignores mundane economic explanations

## Why It Matters

Even if exaggerated, the core observation about increasing artificial content online has become more relevant with generative AI.
