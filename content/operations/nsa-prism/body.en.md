## Overview

PRISM is a program under which the NSA collects internet communications from major tech companies. Leaked by Snowden in 2013.

## Methods

- Direct access to servers of Google, Facebook, Apple, Microsoft, Yahoo
- Operated under Section 702 of FISA
- Companion UPSTREAM programs tapped undersea fiber optic cables

## Consequences

Global diplomatic fallout, tech industry encryption reforms, USA FREEDOM Act (2015).
