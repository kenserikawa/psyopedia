## Overview

Jade Helm 15 was a U.S. military training exercise in 2015 that became the subject of conspiracy theories about martial law.

## The Psyop Angle

Russian-linked social media accounts amplified conspiracy theories about the exercise, making it an early case study in foreign information warfare.

## Legacy

A 2017 study found Russian troll accounts specifically targeted Jade Helm to test techniques later used in the 2016 election.
