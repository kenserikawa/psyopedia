## Overview

In October 1990, a 15-year-old girl known only as "Nayirah" tearfully testified before the U.S. Congressional Human Rights Caucus that she had witnessed Iraqi soldiers removing babies from incubators in Kuwait and leaving them to die on the floor. The testimony was fabricated.

## The Deception

- "Nayirah" was actually Nayirah al-Sabah, daughter of the Kuwaiti ambassador to the United States
- Her testimony was coached by the American PR firm Hill & Knowlton, hired by the Kuwaiti government for $10.8 million
- The incubator story was cited by six U.S. senators as justification for the Gulf War
- President George H.W. Bush repeated the story ten times in the weeks leading to the war
- Amnesty International initially corroborated the claim, later retracted

## Exposure

Journalist John MacArthur exposed the deception in his 1992 book. Human Rights Watch and Amnesty International found no evidence to support the incubator claims.

## Legacy

A textbook case of war propaganda manufactured by a PR firm. Demonstrated how emotional testimony can override fact-checking in the march to war.
