## Overview

ECHELON is a signals intelligence collection and analysis network operated by the Five Eyes alliance. Originally designed to monitor Soviet military communications during the Cold War, it evolved into a global mass surveillance system capable of intercepting phone calls, emails, and fax transmissions worldwide.

## Background

The system dates back to the 1966 UKUSA Agreement. Its existence was long denied until whistleblowers and investigative journalists exposed it in the 1990s.

## Methods

- Satellite interception stations across the globe
- Dictionary computers filtering communications by keywords
- Automated analysis of voice, text, and data traffic
- Sharing of intercepted data among Five Eyes nations to circumvent domestic surveillance laws

## Exposure

The European Parliament conducted an investigation in 2001 confirming ECHELON's existence and its commercial espionage capabilities. Former NSA employees provided testimony.

## Legacy

ECHELON paved the way for the mass surveillance programs later exposed by Edward Snowden in 2013.
